# Connect Signal

Private, end-to-end encrypted messaging with Dex over Signal.

## Link it (about 3 minutes)

1. In Dex, open this app and tap **Pair Signal**. A QR code appears (Dex links as a secondary device).
2. On your phone, open **Signal → Settings → Linked devices → Link new device**.
3. Scan the QR code.
4. Wait for "Linked" — Dex confirms automatically.

## Use cases

- **Private remote control:** command your PC over an encrypted channel.
- **Secure file delivery:** "Send the signed contract to my own number."
- **Quiet reminders:** time-based nudges arrive in Signal.

## Use cases vs. WhatsApp

Pick Signal when you want end-to-end encryption and minimal metadata; pick WhatsApp for reach (most contacts already have it). Dex behaves the same on both.

## Notes

- Signal allows a limited number of linked devices; remove old ones if linking fails.
- Keep your phone reachable occasionally so the linked device stays active.
