# Connect Telegram

Talk to Dex through your own Telegram bot — fast, free, and works from any device signed into Telegram.

## Link it (about 3 minutes)

1. In Telegram, open **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`.
2. Give it a name and a username (must end in `bot`). BotFather replies with a **token** like `123456:ABC-DEF...`.
3. In Dex, open this app, paste the token, and tap **Connect**.
4. Open a chat with your new bot in Telegram and send `hi`. Dex replies.

## Use cases

- **Pocket assistant:** "What's on my desktop right now?" from your phone — Dex answers from your PC.
- **Hands-off automation:** "Open VTOP and check my attendance" while you're out.
- **File delivery:** "Send me the latest screenshot" and Dex pushes it into the chat.
- **Reminders that reach you:** "Remind me at 4pm to submit the form" — the nudge arrives in Telegram.

## Notes

- Keep the bot token private — anyone with it can message your Dex. You can revoke/regenerate it via BotFather (`/revoke`).
- Only you (and people you allow) can pair; strangers who message the bot get a pairing prompt, not access.
