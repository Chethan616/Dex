# Connect WhatsApp

Chat with Dex from WhatsApp, and let Dex send messages and files to you or your contacts — straight from your own number.

## Link it (about 1 minute)

1. In Dex, open this app and tap **Pair WhatsApp**. A QR code appears.
2. On your phone, open **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**.
3. Point your phone at the QR code on screen.
4. Wait for "Linked" — Dex confirms the connection automatically.

That's it. Dex now rides on your existing WhatsApp account; nothing is installed on your phone.

## Use cases

- **Message yourself anything:** "Send my current wallpaper to myself on WhatsApp."
- **Reach a contact:** "WhatsApp Mom that I'll be 20 minutes late."
- **Push a file from the PC:** "Send the invoice PDF on my desktop to Rahul."
- **Remote control:** text Dex "open my email and summarize the top 3" from your phone while away from the PC, and it runs on your desktop.

## Notes

- Keep your phone online occasionally so WhatsApp keeps the linked device alive (same as WhatsApp Web).
- Dex only messages people when you ask it to — it never sends on its own.
- To unlink: WhatsApp → Linked devices → tap the Dex device → Log out.
