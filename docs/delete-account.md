# How to delete your DEX account

Deleting your DEX account removes everything DEX synced for it:
- your PCs and phones;
- your tasks and their chats;
- the commands you sent;
- files on their way between your devices;
- your DEX profile.

Then it removes the account itself. It can't be undone.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open **DEX** on your phone.
2. Go to **Settings**, then scroll to the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete account**, enter your password, and tap **Delete**.

It's done at once, and the app goes back to the sign-in screen.

## If you no longer have the app

- **Install DEX again and sign in:** do this with the same email and password, then follow the steps above.
- **Or ask for it:** write to the developer email shown on DEX's Google Play listing.
  - Send it from the email address of your DEX account.
  - Use the subject "Delete my DEX account".

  Your account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

## What stays

DEX on your PC keeps the tasks and files saved **on your PC**. Delete them there, or uninstall DEX on the PC. Nothing is kept anywhere else. See the [privacy policy](../PRIVACY.md).
